refactor: the Luca retro — retire the XState layer and the bulk telemetry sink, fold the command surface into skills, and make `luca init` prune what it retires

Four waves of removal against surfaces that had accumulated a second copy of themselves: two verdict engines for one transition table, two instruction bodies for every slash command, a local telemetry sink LangSmith had superseded, and six skills that were dead, broken, or duplicates. Every deletion is registered in `RETIRED_ARTIFACTS`, which is what makes it safe — see the breaking-change section. The `todo-ingest` / `goal-brief` skill pair rides in the same branch but is covered by its own changeset.

- **XState retired** (luca-core). The statechart was provably decision-equivalent to the 33-line `PIPELINE_TRANSITIONS` table by its own parity contract — its only edge guard was a requested-step matcher, and the budget guard it was built around was registered but wired to no edge. Meanwhile two engines shipped simultaneously: `luca state advance` ran the XState oracle while the pipeline-guard hook ran the legacy function, with a 169-pair harness existing only to police the duplication. What survives is relocated, not dropped: the transitions table, a literal `STEP_TO_COARSE_PHASE`, `mutateState`, and the fix-loop counters. `decideAdvance` still returns `resultingStep` and `counterUpdate`, because swapping in `checkPipelineGuard` naively would have frozen the fix-loop counters at 0 forever with no test failure. A mermaid golden pins `luca graph` output against drift, and the `xstate` dependency is gone.

- **Telemetry narrowed, not deleted** (luca-core + luca-cli + luca-tools). Full retirement was the wrong shape — two capabilities had no replacement. Retained: the `recall.hit` / `recall.miss` / `recall.utilization` family, the one measurement of the memory layer everything else leans on and the one LangSmith cannot see; and the `trace-insights` Stage A5 join input, which is defined over telemetry JOIN ledger, so retiring telemetry outright would have removed half the input of the skill meant to replace it. The first cut retained both **in name only** — no producer stamped the fields Stage A5 keys on, so the join was dead on arrival while a CLI-leaf test asserted it worked. All seven emit sites now render from one shared constant, and the guard test compiles the artifact manifest and asserts emitted markdown bytes rather than grepping source for tokens. Triage is exempt by design and says so in its own body: its recall runs before complexity is classified and while `currentPhase` is 0, so it emits unattributed rather than fabricating a slug. Signals with a live consumer migrated to the ledger rather than being dropped — fix-loop counters, the learner's signal digest, session-resume's readback. `generateRunId` moved from `telemetry/` to `ledger/`, since it mints `state.sessionId`, the live ledger's grouping key.

- **The 17 slash commands folded into their same-named skills** (luca-tools). Two independent ports populated the two surfaces and were never reconciled — skills from the pre-D-4 `SKILL.md` files, commands from `~/.claude/commands/` as "tighter, more imperative" prompts — so every pair drifted, load-bearingly, in both directions. The fold **direction** was forced, not chosen: the Antigravity harness descriptor installs `{agents, skills}` and not commands, so folding toward the command surface would have deleted Luca from Antigravity entirely, while Claude Code already exposes each `SKILL.md` as `/<name>`. Eleven were mechanical (verbatim duplicates, thin pointers, or strictly contained by their skill). Six had genuinely diverged, and each command-only directive was ported into the skill **before** deletion, because several were the only copy of a load-bearing instruction: `todo-add`'s "execute the returned muninn procedure" delegation (without it a todo validates and never persists), `phase-plan`'s advance to `plan-review`, `phase-discuss`'s canonical `<dir>/context.md` write recipe, `milestone-new`'s `luca workflow reset --confirm` (the skill's bare form is refused by the handler and the refusal was being swallowed), and `phase-execute`'s "do not commit during execute" rule that `STAGE_TOOL_MATRIX.EXECUTING['bash-commit'] === false` makes mandatory. No slash invocation is lost; `defineCommand` and `emitCommand` are retained as compiler contract, not as an invitation.

- **Six skills deleted with evidence** (luca-tools): `phase-insert` (emitted decimal slugs `PHASE_SLUG_RE` rejects), `repo-audit` (shells to a `scripts/` dir that does not exist), `choose` (routes to commands found nowhere), `post-init-tour` and `workflow-save` (zero invocations, both describing themselves inaccurately), and `phase-add` (its default mode was a verbatim duplicate of `note`'s, down to the `mkdir -p` and the hand-edit of the generated `roadmap.md`; `note` is the strict superset and survives). Two further planned deletions were **refused** with evidence: `session-resume` and `luca-telemetry-report` were slated on the premise that their value died with telemetry, but the preceding wave had already migrated session-resume's readback to the ledger and deliberately retained the recall corpus — executing those todos as written would have undone it.

- **Pipeline-legality repairs** (luca-cli + luca-core + luca-tools). Illegal `luca state advance` targets removed from shipped instruction bodies, along with the `2>/dev/null || true` masking: a swallowed advance strands the pipeline at the prior step and every downstream artifact write is then gate-blocked for a reason that never surfaces. The defect was larger than reported — `phase-execute` never advanced at all between steps 0 and 9, so `verify.json` and `audits/*.md` were being written at `execute`, where `STEP_ARTIFACTS` forbids them. A **build-time legality test** now compiles the full artifact manifest and asserts every `--to-step` literal against `PIPELINE_TRANSITIONS`, keyed by the stage each body owns; it is the causal fix for the drift class, and it carries no opt-out (the first attempt's exemption tripwire was structurally incapable of firing). The stage-gate bash parser was rewritten onto real shell tokenization — it was simultaneously too loose (a first-match `findIndex` missed a second advance in an `&&` chain) and too strict (it matched command text, blocking an `echo` string containing "luca state advance").

- **`luca roadmap add-phase` / `remove-phase`** (luca-cli), so phase registration and renumbering are deterministic CLI operations rather than prose that `mkdir -p`s directories and hand-edits generated `roadmap.md`. Both are contract violations: `LUCA_DIR_CONTRACT` declares `roadmap.md` generated output, and a raw `mkdir` bypasses `PHASE_SLUG_RE` — which is exactly how `phase-insert` shipped decimal directories the stage gate rejected. The verbs landed in wave 1 **with zero callers**; wave 4 migrated `note`, `phase-remove`, `milestone-gaps`, and `milestone-new` onto them, and a guard test asserts the migration against compiled bytes plus bans the `mkdir`/roadmap-edit prose corpus-wide so it cannot return under a new name.

- **`workflow-authoring` skill** (luca-tools) — authoring guidance for Claude Code's harness-provided Workflow tool, teaching four techniques Luca paid for in production (convergence promotion, perspective diversity, bounded iteration, output pruning) and citing the running implementation of each, with every `path:line` asserted to resolve by a sibling test. To be plain about scope: **nothing ships in this repo for it** — no dependency, no executor, no `defineWorkflow` artifact kind. The planned port of the pipeline steps onto a Workflow was never executed; this skill documents the existing implementations and never modifies them.

## BREAKING for existing installs

Upgrading does not merely stop shipping these — `luca init` now actively removes them from `~/.claude`. Prior to this, `init` only ever copied, so retired artifacts persisted and stayed invocable against infrastructure that no longer exists. The prune quarantines rather than deletes, refuses to touch a still-bundled name, and prunes **nothing** when the bundle cannot be enumerated (the first cut failed dangerous there and could evict live skills). `luca doctor`'s stray-repo path consults the same list, so a pre-v13 per-repo copy is quarantined rather than left loadable. One subtlety this depends on: the compiler now emits an empty-but-real `commands/` bucket, because "deliberately empty" and "not built" are otherwise indistinguishable to the prune, and it must fail closed on the latter.

- **Six skills removed**: `phase-insert`, `repo-audit`, `choose`, `post-init-tour`, `workflow-save`, `phase-add`.
- **All 17 slash commands removed** — the surface is now empty. Every one is still invocable as `/<name>` via its skill in Claude Code, so this is a de-duplication rather than a capability loss.
- **luca-core exports removed**: `machineVerdict` and its `MachineVerdict` / `MachineVerdictInput` types, and `pipelineDefinitionJson`. `renderPipelineMermaid`, `pipelineGraphEdges`, `createPipelineActorHandle`, `REWORK_EDGE_CAPS` and the `PipelineActorHandle` / `PipelineActorSnapshot` / `CounterUpdate` types survive under the same names, relocated out of `state/machine/` into `state/helpers/` and `state/configs/`.
- **Telemetry schemas removed**: `SatisfactionSignalMetaSchema`, `ClassifierOverrideMetaSchema`, `FailureDumpMetaSchema`, `PrOutcomeMetaSchema`, `OverrideSourceSchema` and their types. `.luca/telemetry/*.jsonl` no longer carries satisfaction signals, outcome KPIs beyond the artifact-derived ones, PR outcomes, classifier overrides, or subagent correlation.
- **`telemetryHooks[]` removed** from the `defineAgent` / `defineSubagent` guidance schema and from the compiled-prompt surface.
- **CLI verbs removed**: `luca telemetry pr-outcome` (retired outright with the bulk sink) and `luca telemetry new-run` (superseded by `luca state new-run`, which mints the same `state.sessionId`-shaped id).

Bump is `minor` rather than `major`: `@alecsibilia/luca-core`, `-cli`, and `-tools` are all `private: true` and never publish, so their export surfaces carry no external contract, and the fixed group keeps the published `@alecsibilia/luca` in lockstep. The repo stays in changesets `pre` mode on tag `alpha` — this batch does not exit it, and nothing here ships stable. The two removed `luca telemetry` leaves are the one item that would argue for `major` against a released 13.0.1; they are called out above so the decision is on the record rather than implied.

Instruction-body edits reach installed harnesses via `bun run build` + a `luca init` re-run.
